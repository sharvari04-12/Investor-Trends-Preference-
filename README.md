>>>Investor Trends & Preferences Dashboard — Power BI<<<
An interactive Power BI dashboard analysing investment behaviour, preferences, and patterns across 160 survey respondents — covering investment avenues, savings 
objectives, gender-based choices, and information sources.

>>>Problem Statement
Understanding how individuals make investment decisions is critical for financial advisors, fintech companies, and policymakers. This dashboard analyses a survey of
160 investors to answer:

>>>Key Insights from the Dashboard
| Metric | Finding |
|---|---|
| **Total Investors Surveyed** | 160 |
| **Average Age** | 27.80 years |
| **Most Preferred Investment** | Mutual Fund (45%) |
| **Most Common Duration** | 3–5 years |
| **Most Common Saving Objective** | Retirement Plan (96 respondents) |
| **Most Common Reason for Investment** | Capital Appreciation |

 >>>Key Analytical Findings

- **Mutual Funds dominate** across all age groups and genders — 45% of all investors prefer them
- **Retirement planning** is the primary savings objective regardless of age, even among 21–25 year olds
- **Financial Consultants** are trusted more than the Internet as an information source — suggesting older demographic influence
- **Female investors** are slightly more risk-averse, favouring Mutual Funds and Fixed Deposits over Equity
- **Capital Appreciation** is the top investment reason, indicating growth-oriented rather than income-oriented investor behaviour
- **3–5 year horizon** is most common — investors are medium-term focused, not short-term traders
